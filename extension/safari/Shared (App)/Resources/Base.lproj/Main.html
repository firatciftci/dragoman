dragoman

Translate any page, right where it is.

  1. Open Safari and tap the page menu in the address bar.
  2. Choose Manage Extensions and turn on dragoman.
  3. Tap dragoman in that menu and choose Always Allow on Every Website.

Turn on dragoman in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

dragoman is on. You can manage it in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.
